Scientific methodology & provenance

Cosmic Atlas v4 separates measured catalog quantities, literature-derived distances, cosmology-derived values, imported catalog records, and illustrative geometry. It is an educational scientific visualization and data-exploration product, not a survey reconstruction or precision astrometry package.

Data tiers

  • Observational: ICRS positions and selected astrometric / spectroscopic quantities transcribed from SIMBAD/CDS records and their cited literature. Several stellar astrometric values trace Gaia or Hipparcos-family catalog references.
  • Literature distance: selected distances use published geometric or standard-candle measurements where redshift would be a poor distance estimator (Local Group, Galactic Centre, Centaurus A).
  • Model-derived: cosmological redshift, lookback time and the 3C 273 comoving distance use a flat Planck-2018-like ΛCDM model (H0=67.4 km/s/Mpc, Ωm=0.315).
  • Imported catalog: v4 can ingest normalized physical or angular-only records generated by the included Gaia/BSC/OpenNGC scripts. Imported records are kept distinct from the curated core.
  • Illustrative context: Milky Way particles, Local Group textures, cosmic-web filaments, macrostructure landmark centers, and the CMB texture are procedural visualizations. They are explicitly not survey maps.

Distance policy

For high-S/N nearby parallaxes, v4 uses the direct inverse-parallax distance for visualization and reports the measurement provenance. For objects where that approximation is unsuitable, a literature distance is adopted and named. Distances remain rounded for an atlas-scale display.

Coordinate system

Catalog directions are ICRS/J2000-equivalent sky coordinates as reported by the cited records. The 3D renderer is observer-centered at Sol and uses a nonlinear radial transform only for display; angular directions are preserved.
